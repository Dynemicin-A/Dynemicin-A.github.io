Hi, I am Xuanhao.

I am an undergraduate researcher interested in generative models, multimodal learning, and AI for scientific research. I enjoy building careful experiments and reproducible systems for complex generative tasks.

I am currently pursuing a B.E. in Biopharmaceutical Technology at China Pharmaceutical University, expected in 2027.

About me

My current work is centered on how generative models use conditioning information: adapting guidance to a prompt, learning effectively across generation stages, and evaluating generation quality. I am particularly interested in diffusion models, audio generation, and multimodal AI.

I am also interested in machine learning engineering and high-performance computing. I work primarily with Python and have experience with C.
