Current research

Ongoing work on RSI and automated research

Meta-level adaptation

How can an agent improve its experiment-selection and tool-use policies from its own research trajectories? I am studying a two-level setup that separates the experimental loop from an editable research controller.

Open-ended skill discovery

How can search recover useful capabilities from candidates that initially perform poorly? I am exploring novelty-aware archives of agent variants and executable skills, retaining experimental context for later reuse.

Measuring recursive progress

Do controller updates produce cumulative gains beyond the problems used to select them? I am designing parent–descendant evaluations that select candidates on validation tasks and measure gains over a fixed parent on held-out tasks under matched compute budgets.
