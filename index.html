Research experience

Shanghai Institute of Materia Medica

Joint-Latent Diffusion for Small-Molecule Generation

Developed an unconditional 3D molecular generation framework on GEOM-Drugs, combining UAE-3D and Uni-Mol latent representations.

  • Implemented a dual-head diffusion Transformer and a FiLM-based joint decoder; evaluated loss-weight ablations using molecular validity and stability.
  • Optimized feature caching and data transfer for training and evaluation.
